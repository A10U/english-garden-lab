# Articulation image credits

These SVG files are **unmodified** copies of the published originals linked below. Their bytes were checked against Wikimedia Commons image SHA1 metadata; SHA256 hashes are recorded in `ATTRIBUTION.json`. Thai captions in `articulation-images.js` are separate project-authored text and are not part of the original images.

These are schematic articulations, not measurements of a learner. The /i/ diagram illustrates tongue position when explaining /iː/; it does not show vowel duration. The lateral-l image compares two /l/ configurations.
